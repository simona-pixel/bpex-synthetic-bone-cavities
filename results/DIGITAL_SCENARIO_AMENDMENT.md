# Illustration source amendment

The new digital scenario was first executed on VSD subject 002. Its outputs are preserved under initial_VSD names for internal traceability. The VSD mesh licence is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. After inspecting the source licences, the publication illustration and hollow-segment example were transferred to the lexically first evaluable Figshare tibia, case 102480, whose source record specifies CC BY 4.0. This change concerns illustration reuse and the digital demonstration only. It does not alter sensitivity cases, configurations, results or independent-validation denominators, and was not selected on geometric performance.

The revised figures attribute Keast, Bonacci and Fox, Figshare version 4, doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.20454462.v4. They are newly drawn from source and synthetic geometry. Source meshes are not redistributed in the journal code archive.
