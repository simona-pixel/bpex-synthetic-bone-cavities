## Journal preparation requirements

Official IOP revision guidance: https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/publishing-support/authors/authoring-for-journals/writing-submitting-revised-article/

Official linked revision checklist: https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Revision-checklist.pdf

The official checklist, not the unavailable message attachment, was inspected. It requests a point-by-point response, highlighted PDF, clean editable source with editable equations/tables, clean PDF, individual source figures and supplementary material. The original submission did not opt into double-anonymous review. A clean and a highlighted manuscript are prepared with the existing author list.

The BPEX article-type page describes Notes as normally no more than 4000 words. Word counts including references and tables are recorded by the document builder; displayed equation tokens are additionally editable Word math.
